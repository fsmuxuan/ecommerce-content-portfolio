# Platform detail-page adapters and competitor-reference protocol

## Non-negotiable baseline

All platforms use the same confirmed fact library and approved real-image catalog. The platform changes the order, density, call to action, and media mix; it never changes what can be truthfully claimed. Keep the company 60-full-width-unit title cap and the picture-led proof sequence.

## 1688: procurement confidence

Prioritize the exact product term, selection/specification, supply or customization boundary, workshop/packing proof when available, and a quote-oriented CTA. Put B2B decision information early: applicable buyer, options, MOQ or lead time only if confirmed, and the information needed for quotation.

## Taobao: fast consumer decision

Lead with visible benefit and fit, then product details, variants/size, service reassurance, and a low-friction purchase CTA. Use a faster image rhythm and fewer procurement paragraphs. Do not import unverified consumer claims, guarantees, or comparison claims.

## Douyin Shop: video-to-detail continuity

The first detail images must continue the hook demonstrated in the short video or live room: situation, visible result, and product close-up. Put the one buyer concern answered by the video near the top, use concise visual captions, then selection and service information. Detail content supports conversion but must not promise results the video or facts cannot prove.

## Baidu AiCaigou: search-to-inquiry qualification

Use the precise product term, applicable industry/use, visible product evidence, specifications/options, supplier/service boundary, and contact/quote CTA in a clear search-friendly hierarchy. Retain enough confirmed readable text for search intent and qualification; do not turn the page into an image-only poster.

## Researching public competitor pages safely

When the user asks for competitor reference, research 3–5 current public pages for the same product category and target platform. Record only this structured observation in `03_竞品页面参考摘要.md`:

- URL, platform, observed date, product category, and public access status;
- buyer question answered first;
- module order, image density, image role, copy density, and CTA placement;
- a reusable *method* hypothesis, clearly labelled as a hypothesis;
- what is excluded because it is competitor wording, imagery, price, certification, client information, or unverified claim.

Never copy or publish competitor text, images, layout artwork, customer cases, prices, reviews, supplier claims, or trademarks. Do not treat a competitor's claim as a fact about the user's product. Screenshots, if made, are internal research notes only and never source material for the deliverable.
